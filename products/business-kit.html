Know what you’re buying.

What do I receive?

A digital bundle of Excel, editable Word and PDF resources, with quick-start guidance. Check the Etsy listing for the exact file inventory before purchase. No physical item is shipped.

Can I customize, save and print it?

Yes—fill in editable fields, save your own copies and print suitable documents. Workbook input cells are editable; formula cells are protected to help prevent accidental damage.

What software do I need?

Use compatible software for the Excel and Word files and a PDF reader for PDFs. For AI lead research, bring your own web-enabled AI tool. An AI subscription, Excel license and automatic integrations are not included. Check compatibility details on Etsy.

Does it guarantee leads or sales?

No. These are tools for organizing your work. You provide the information, verify research and decide what to do next. Sales depend on your offer, market and outreach.

Is this the right fit for me?

It is designed for a small business owner who wants reusable files instead of another monthly software bill. If you need an app that runs outreach automatically or syncs with your shop, this kit does not provide that.
