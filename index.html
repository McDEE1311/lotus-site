Rooted locally

The lotus grows in the muddiest water—and reaches the clearest light.

Lotus Intelligence LLC was founded in Decherd, Tennessee, to bring practical websites, local search, automation, and business monitoring to local businesses that rarely have access to enterprise-level technology.

We work with service businesses—HVAC, plumbing, electrical, pest control, roofing, real estate, and landscaping—across Winchester, Decherd, Estill Springs, Tullahoma, Manchester, and Sewanee.
