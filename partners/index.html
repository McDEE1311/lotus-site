The next stage

What the next stage requires.

Suitable GPU host systems

Host platforms that can run the owned RTX 3060s for the intended AI workloads.

Reliable networking

Connectivity suited to rental and hosting workloads.

Power protection

Protection against outages and power events for installed equipment.

Cooling

Thermal capacity for additional systems running under sustained load.

Time and resources to develop practical AI services for small businesses.

Equipment partners

Partners who can supply or co-locate hardware under clear written terms.

12-month development plan

Milestones, not promises.

These are planned milestones. Timing depends on equipment and funding. They are not a forecast of revenue or profitability.

  1. Months 1–3

    • Record pilot earnings, utilization, and costs monthly
    • Publish first verified, dated pilot figures
    • Select host platform for the RTX 3060s

  2. Months 4–6

    • Install first host system
    • Add power protection and networking upgrades
    • Benchmark the 3060 systems on target workloads

  3. Months 7–9

    • Bring benchmarked capacity online in stages
    • Address cooling for sustained load
    • Pilot one AI service with local businesses

  4. Months 10–12

    • Review results against verified records
    • Decide on further expansion based on data
    • Report progress to partners
