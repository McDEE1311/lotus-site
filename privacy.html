Lotus LotiE Publisher

Privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

About this policy

Lotus Intelligence LLC, based in Decherd, Tennessee, operates Lotus LotiE Publisher to publish approved content to its own Facebook Page. This policy covers that internal publishing tool and requests about its data. It does not describe the data practices of Harmony Hub or other Lotus products.

Information the publisher handles

  • Facebook authorization credentials, including access tokens used to connect the publisher to our Page. We do not ask for or store Facebook passwords in the publisher.
  • Page identifiers and names used to verify the publishing destination.
  • Post text and any media submitted for publication.
  • Operational records such as campaign identifiers, approval fingerprints, publishing status, timestamps, returned post identifiers, and error information.
  • The email address and information you provide if you contact us about privacy or deletion.

The current publisher is not designed to collect follower profiles, read private messages, or operate other businesses' Pages. Do not include sensitive personal information in a privacy request unless it is necessary to identify the information concerned.

How information is used and shared

We use this information to authenticate with Meta, verify our Page, publish approved content, prevent duplicate posts, troubleshoot failures, and respond to requests. Approved post content is sent to Meta and is intended for publication on Facebook. Meta processes information under its own policies, including its Privacy Policy.

We do not sell personal information handled by this tool. Hosting, email, and infrastructure providers may process information needed to provide their services. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Storage, security, and retention

Publishing credentials and operational records are maintained on systems controlled by Lotus. Credentials are kept separate from public website files and are not intended to appear in posts or public source code. Access is restricted to authorized operators and software that needs it.

Credentials are retained while the connection is in use and replaced or removed when access is revoked or the tool is retired. Publishing records are retained as needed for operation, duplicate prevention, troubleshooting, and legitimate recordkeeping. Contact correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and any related obligations. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Data deletion instructions

Email info@lotusintell.com with the subject LotiE Publisher data deletion. Identify the information you want deleted and include a relevant post link or the email address you used to contact us, if applicable. Never send passwords or access tokens.

We may ask for enough information to verify that you are entitled to make the request. We will review the request and explain the action taken or any information that must be retained for legal or security reasons. Deletion from Lotus systems does not automatically remove Facebook posts or information held independently by Meta; tell us if your request concerns content on our Page so we can review it separately.

The Page administrator can also revoke the app's access through Facebook's business integration settings. Revoking access stops future authorized access but does not by itself delete existing published posts or local records.
